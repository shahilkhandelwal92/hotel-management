# StayOS Phase 8 Real Hotel Controlled Pilot Execution

## 1. Scope & Execution Strategy
Phase 8 simulates and validates complete real-world multi-department operations across Front Desk, Housekeeping, Cashiering, Food & Beverage, Engineering Maintenance, Multi-Store Inventory, and Night Audit.

---

## 2. Tested & Verified Workflows
* **Housekeeping:** Room turnover board, minibar inspection & automated guest billing.
* **Front Desk:** Search, room allocation, check-in with advance deposit, in-stay room move, and zero-balance checkout.
* **Split Folios:** Window 1–4 charge distribution with exact Decimal balance conservation.
* **Cashiering:** Cash float open, cash drops, paid-outs, blind count close, and managerial variance approval.
* **Engineering:** Maintenance work orders, plant asset catalog, Out-of-Order room isolation and release.
* **Stores & Inventory:** Multi-store requisitions, dispatch `IN_TRANSIT`, receiving and stock conservation.
* **Night Audit:** Revenue aggregation, day locking immutability, and date rollover.
